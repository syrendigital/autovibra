Got Questions?

FREQUENTLY ASKED
QUESTIONS

Do you come to me or do I drop my car off?▾
100% mobile — we come to your home, apartment, workplace, or wherever your car is parked in Salt Lake City and surrounding Utah areas. No drop-off needed.
How do I get a quote?▾
Fill out our free quote form above. Tell us your vehicle type, condition, and the service you're interested in. We'll text you a custom quote within minutes.
Why don't you show pricing on the website?▾
Every vehicle is different. A lightly used sedan and a heavily used SUV with kids and pets require completely different levels of work. We give you a custom quote based on your actual vehicle and condition so you always get a fair, accurate price.
How long does a detail take?▾
An exterior wash typically takes 1–1.5 hours. Interior cleaning takes 2–3 hours. A full detail can take 3–5 hours depending on vehicle size and condition. We'll give you an accurate time estimate with your quote.
What areas do you serve in Utah?▾
We serve Salt Lake City and the entire Wasatch Front including Sandy, Draper, South Jordan, Murray, West Valley City, Provo, Orem, Lehi, and Ogden. Not sure if we cover your area? Text us at (801) 948-0819.
What is hybrid ceramic spray sealant?▾
It's a professional paint protection product combining traditional wax with ceramic SiO2 technology. It creates a hydrophobic layer that repels water, blocks UV rays, and protects your paint from road grime for several months. Included in every exterior and full detail service.
How do I pay?▾
We accept all major credit cards, debit cards, Venmo, Zelle, and cash. A small deposit is required when booking to secure your appointment slot. The remaining balance is due at time of service.
